Three simulations to play with

These go with the video. When the talk says "pause here", come to the matching simulation, take your time, then go back to the video. Drag any 3D view to turn it. Use the sliders to move forward and backward in time.

1 A train of three pulses 2 Build the steady state 3 The hidden echo at TR/2

Teaching illustrations, not scanner simulations. Simulation 1: spins on a row in a steady gradient, relaxation ignored. Simulations 2 and 3: one voxel with approximate 1.5 T relaxation times, perfect pulses, alternating pulse phase, ideal balanced gradients. Steady-state dynamics: Scheffler K, MRM 2003;49:781–783. Hidden echo: Scheffler K, Hennig J, MRM 2003;49:395–397. Stimulated echo: Hahn EL, Phys Rev 1950;80:580–594. Companion to the narrated talk on Substack.
